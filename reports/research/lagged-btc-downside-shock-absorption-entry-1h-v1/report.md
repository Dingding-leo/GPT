## Frozen signal

For a completed BTC hour `u`, the shock threshold was `r_u <= -3 × sigma_u`, where `sigma_u` was `1.4826 × MAD` of the preceding 168 completed BTC hourly log returns. A shock was absorptive only when its lower-wick fraction was at least 0.50. At a completed daily decision `t`, the latest absorptive shock aged 24–168H qualified only if BTC had closed above the shock close and had not made a lower low after the shock.

For each target, this common lagged BTC state could trigger a cash-to-long entry only while the target’s own 2,160H endpoint trend was positive. Once long, the target’s own slow trend controlled the exit.

The state did not collapse OOS; it expanded from 0.98% to 5.65% of daily decisions and from one to thirteen independently referenced state-supporting shocks. That cannot rescue the family because the frozen training-only support gate failed before OOS inspection. The exact architecture therefore lacks enough historical event support to justify activation, and its apparent OOS availability is post-declaration diagnostic evidence only.

With the candidate inactive, it skipped all 23 BTC and all 15 ETH B1 positive-trend regimes. It omitted +97.99% BTC and +86.58% ETH arithmetic market return while saving only 2.25% and 1.50% in fees. The exposure/fee decomposition reconstructed candidate-minus-B1 arithmetic net exactly to `1e-12`.

## Repaired discrepancy

The initial diagnostic counted daily absorption-state decisions but did not distinguish repeated daily decisions generated by the same shock event. The terminal diagnostic now records unique state-supporting events and maximum event concentration. Training's six state decisions all came from one BTC shock. No feature, support rule, activation decision, position, return, fee, benchmark, uncertainty gate, or verdict changed.

## Remaining blocker and next experiment

**Remaining blocker:** event support and state independence. A daily state can appear to have multiple observations while being generated by one underlying shock, making conservative training activation impossible.

**Next strategy experiment:** preregister one own-history-only slow-trend drawdown-budget architecture that changes position size continuously from 0 to 1 using a fixed causal ratio of current 2,160H trend margin to trailing 720H realised volatility, with a no-trade band to control turnover. Use one fixed functional form, no fitted threshold, no market-specific rule, one candidate and the same bilateral breadth and moving-block uncertainty gates.
